# Two-minute demo script

Record at 1280 × 800, browser full screen, live site. Say each line while doing it.

**0:00 – 0:15 · The problem**
Start screen. "A Mars crew has seven hours of suit time. If the rover fails far from base, they walk home. A.R.E.S. tells them, before they leave, where that is safe."

**0:15 – 0:40 · The data**
Open Overview. Switch CTX, THEMIS night, HiRISE, slope on and off.
"Every layer is NASA data: CTX and HiRISE images from MRO, THEMIS thermal from Mars Odyssey, heights from MOLA and HRSC. Bright at night means rock; red means too steep for the rover."

**0:40 – 1:05 · The route**
Point at the five sites and the routes.
"Five sites, one per sol, lowest risk first. The routes bend round steep and rough ground. Where no route exists, the map says so in red and the day is refused."

**1:05 – 1:35 · A day on Mars**
Open Sols, Sol 6. Show the countdown, then the tether check.
"Sol 6 is the crater rim. Retrieval stays locked until both crew are tethered. The clock is Mars local time; at 12:09 they must leave."

**1:35 – 1:50 · When it goes wrong**
Sol 5: trigger the gas alert, then abort. Open Sol 7.
"A gas alert aborts Sol 5. Sol 7 picks the least finished site and repeats it."

**1:50 – 2:00 · Close**
Mission, "Run all five sols".
"Plan every step to come home. Built on NASA data, with the limits stated on the map."

Checklist before recording: sign in as Commander; clear old state (new private window); battery 100% on Sol 7 before switching to Plan B.
